# Culture Compass

Your companion for going deeper into culture — before, during, and after you experience it. Get spoiler-free context on the film, book, or series you're about to watch or read: the story behind how it was made, without giving away the plot or its meaning. Dive into an album, a novel, or a work of art once you're ready to really understand it. Discover what's on — exhibitions, concerts, and cultural events near you. Culture Compass helps you approach culture with curiosity, at whatever stage you're in.

| Skill | Description |
